What monday sidekick does as your context-aware AI assistant monday sidekick is an AI assistant built directly into the AI Workspace. It connects to your work data and apps to help you think, plan, create, and act through conversation. As it understands your boards, docs, teammates, and connected apps, every response reflects your real work rather than a generic chatbot reply. Sidekick is accessible at 3 levels: Account-wide : from the left panel Board-level : from the AI star icon at the top of any board Item-level : from the star icon next to any item, or by selecting up to 10 items for batch actions Voice mode adds a hands-free option that uses conversational speech to go back and forth to the point of confirmation before anything impactful happens. Work with boards, docs, and people in full context A few examples show how Sidekick’s context awareness plays out day to day. Analyze board data : ask questions like “How many items per priority are there?” or “What’s overdue this week?” and get instant answers drawn from the columns, statuses, dates, and assignees on your live board. Update work : create, edit, delete, and duplicate items, groups, columns, and labels through conversation. Instead of clicking through menus, just tell Sidekick “Add a new item called ‘Update homepage content’ with high priority and assign it to Sarah,” and it handles the rest. Read sub-items : access column information and updates at the sub-item level, so you can drill into the details of any item without navigating away from the chat. Flag what needs attention : Sidekick catches delays, suggests follow-ups, highlights trends, and recommends actions based on team activity, identifying issues on its own rather than waiting for you to ask. Notify teammates : tag people, send updates, trigger follow-ups, and escalate issues without leaving the chat. When you need to loop someone in, sidekick handles the communication directly. Generate documents : create project plans with owners and deadlines, strategic documents, reports, and on-brand visuals, all ready to use inside your workspace. Sidekick respects existing permissions. It only accesses and modifies data a team member is already authorized to see, so information stays within its intended boundaries. Without leaving monday AI Workspace, you can: Send Slack messages Compose and send emails, including HTML-formatted emails with file attachments up to 5 MB Check Google Calendar availability Schedule meetings Sidekick pulls information from multiple sources to do this, including your monday content, attached files, web search, and advanced language models, so answers land in one place instead of spread across tabs and applications. Generate content, images, project plans, and dashboards Sidekick’s creative and building capabilities extend well beyond answering questions. Content generation : write emails, project briefs, strategic documents, and reports tailored to your work context, drawing on what sidekick already knows about the project. Image and visual creation : generate on-brand visuals and images ready to use for presentations, project documentation , internal communications, and client-facing materials. Workflow creation : build workflows, automations, dashboards, and forms from a simple request; describe what you need, and sidekick sets it up. App and agent guidance : when your request goes beyond what Sidekick handles directly, it points you to monday vibe to build an app or to the agent builder to create a custom agent, carrying your context along. How monday agents execute real work for every team Agents are workers embedded directly into your monday AI Workspace, handling real work on your behalf across departments. Where Sidekick is your personal assistant you chat with in the moment, agents are specialist workers you configure once and then leave to run — scoring leads, triaging tickets , analyzing risks, and summarizing meetings on their own schedule. The same core capabilities power every agent: Knowledge : agents use the docs, PDFs, boards, and forms you define as context, so every action is grounded in your guidelines, not generic training data. Actions : agents execute across every part of work — flagging what needs attention, updating items, changing statuses, and reassigning work. Integrations : agents keep work in sync across your connected apps, pulling context and taking actions without manual handoffs. 24/7 autonomy : agents work with no limits on time zone, volume, language, or day of the week, following up, generating content, and logging results as they go. Guardrails : permissions you control and a clear record of what an agent did and why, plus what happens next. How to build a custom monday AI agent Building a custom agent takes 3 steps, with no technical expertise required. Describe what you need : define the agent’s role, the work it should handle, and when it should execute. Be specific about the triggers, the data it should monitor, and the actions it should take. Make it yours : connect the knowledge sources (docs, PDFs, boards) and integrations the agent needs — this is what grounds it in your work rather than generic responses. See it in action : test the agent, make adjustments, and watch how work gets done. Iterate until the outputs match your expectations before deploying at scale. What monday MCP does and which external AI tools it supports MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that connects external AI assistants to the monday AI Workspace, so they can securely read and act on your data. monday’s MCP capability is the hosted server behind that connection, meaning you can ask Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, or Cursor to create items, run analyses, update pipelines, and generate reports directly from their own chat interfaces. Connecting takes 3 steps: Install the MCP app from the monday marketplace (requires account admin). Ask your AI assistant to perform any action on your work directly from its chat. Get instant results as your AI assistant updates data, creates items, provides analysis, and acts on your behalf. All monday.com plans include MCP at no extra cost, though standard API limits apply. Supported AI clients including Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, and Cursor monday MCP works with the following AI clients, each connecting to your workspace data through the MCP protocol: ChatGPT : OpenAI’s generative AI assistant Claude : Anthropic’s AI assistant Cursor : AI-powered code editor with integrated assistant Copilot Studio : Microsoft’s platform for creating custom AI assistants le Chat : Mistral AI’s AI agent Figma Make : Figma’s AI design systems generator Gemini CLI : Google’s command-line interface open-source agent Other MCP-compatible assistants should also work, though individual setup steps may vary. Each team member authorizes MCP individually via OAuth, and it only accesses the data that person can already see on monday AI Workspace — the same permission boundaries that apply in the UI carry over to MCP. A few prompts to try right away: “Create a board called ‘Website refresh’ with status, assignee, priority, and due date columns.” “Find all items marked as ‘Stuck’ across all my boards and provide details about each one.” “Add a comment to the blog post creation item: ‘Waiting for final copy from the content team.'” AI blocks, workflows, and automations built into your boards AI blocks are pre-built AI functions you add to your workflows to automate column-level work. Each handles a specific kind of task and can even recommend the following based on what you need: Categorize : AI reads text in a designated field and selects the most appropriate label from your status or dropdown column. Works best with 3–10 distinct labels that are easy to tell apart. Summarize : condenses complex text into shorter, scannable summaries with key points — useful for turning lengthy feedback, meeting notes, project descriptions, or support tickets into actionable takeaways. Extract information : automatically pulls details from PDFs, text columns, monday docs, and images — names, invoice numbers, dates, text drawn from images — directly into board columns. Supported formats include .pdf, .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .docx, .xlsx, and .pptx. Translate : translates text into any specified language from a wide variety of options, breaking language barriers for teams working across markets. Detect sentiment : identifies emotional cues from text, categorizing as positive, negative, or neutral — useful for customer feedback, support tickets, survey responses, and product reviews. Write : generates text based on a prompt with configurable tone and length, useful for drafting descriptions, responses, standardized content, or item summaries. Improve text : refines text for a more professional tone, correcting grammar and typos. Works with any language but performs best with English. Custom block : write any prompt in your own words and AI creates a custom automation that fits your workflow, handling situations the pre-set actions above don’t cover. AI blocks work with text, date, number, dropdown, people, and status columns. Apply them from any column’s three-dot menu using Autofill with AI: click it, choose an action, preview results, and save. Try monday agents Frequently asked questions Does monday.com use my data to train AI models? No, monday.com doesn't use your customer data or content to train its AI models and does not allow third parties to do so. While monday.com may temporarily access your data (for up to 60 days) to monitor and improve services, it never shares that data with third parties or uses it for model training. You can opt out by contacting [email protected] . What AI models does monday.com use? The platform primarily uses Microsoft Azure OpenAI as its AI model provider and is actively integrating models available through AWS Bedrock, such as Mistral and Anthropic, while also supporting connections to Claude and Gemini through its open AI infrastructure. Is monday AI HIPAA compliant? Yes. monday AI is HIPAA compliant, and the platform also holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 27701 certifications along with GDPR compliance. What happens when I run out of AI credits on monday.com? Every monday.com account receives 500 free AI credits per month. You can purchase additional credits as needed and monitor consumption through the AI Cost Center, which tracks usage per team and lets admins set limits and alerts. Can I bring my own AI model or use a custom API key with monday AI? Currently, monday AI does not support bringing your own model or using custom API keys within the platform itself. monday MCP connects external AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, and Cursor to your monday.com workspace data using the open Model Context Protocol instead. How do I opt out of monday.com's temporary content access? Submit a request via [email protected] or through your account representative, specifying which account(s) to opt out if your email is linked to multiple accounts. Only admins can submit this request, and processing may take up to 5 business days. What is the difference between Sidekick Lite, Sidekick Plus, and Super Sidekick? Sidekick Lite (included with Standard and Pro plans) provides 5 messages per day within a single workspace, while Sidekick Plus (included with Enterprise, purchasable for Standard and Pro) provides 100 messages per day with account-wide context. Super Sidekick (for Enterprise, Pro, and Standard) will provide 500 messages per day, also with account-wide context.

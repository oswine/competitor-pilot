Data Center Security ClickUp customer data is hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS), which is certified SOC 2 Type 2. AWS maintains an impressive list of reports, certifications, and third party assessments to ensure complete and ongoing state-of-the-art data center security . AWS infrastructure is housed in Amazon-controlled data centers throughout the world, and the data centers themselves are secured with a variety of physical controls to prevent unauthorized access.

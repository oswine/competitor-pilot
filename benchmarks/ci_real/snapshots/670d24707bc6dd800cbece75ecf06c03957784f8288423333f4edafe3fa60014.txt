It will store your docs in an organized way, and you can invite Doc Makers and Editors into your workspace to collaborate all in one place. For administrators, this is the place where you'll configure your team's settings and manage your account. What is a 'Doc Maker'? + Doc Makers have the power to create new docs and templates, create and manage folders to organize the workspace. Within docs, Doc Makers can administer tasks such as adding pages, changing doc and page titles and icons, adjusting the timezone, configuring doc and page locking, etc. Editors can edit content and collaborate in existing docs & pages, including adding tables & views, constructing formulas & buttons, adding and configuring Packs & automations, etc. Who do I pay for and why? + When upgrading a free workspace to a paid plan, you get access to role-based permissions: each member can be either a Doc Maker (Admin), a Doc Maker, or an Editor. As you grow into Superhuman Docs, you can invite additional members to become Editors or Doc Makers in your workspace. Editors and Viewers are always free. At the end of each billing cycle, you will be billed for the workspace based on the number of Doc Makers in it. Any changes to the number of Doc Makers in the workspace mid-cycle are automatically prorated. What is Docs AI? + Docs AI is the native assistant built into Superhuman Docs. Ask it to build your next tracker, table or dashboard, surface insights from across your workspace, or refine your documents to move faster with your team. Is Docs AI included in all plans? + Whilst in Beta, Docs AI is included in any paid plan with unlimited access (fair use policy applies). The Free plan includes a limited number of trials. Can I use Superhuman Docs via an MCP server? + Superhuman Docs is available via MCP for any Doc Maker, with limited trials on free plans and unlimited access on any paid plan (fair use policy applies). Can I give access to people outside? + Yes! Whenever you share a doc with someone outside of your workspace, they will be added as a Guest Editor (though you can turn this ability off if you'd like). Guest Editors can edit content in individual docs shared with them, but cannot add and manage pages, or browse the doc's workspace or folder. Guest Editors are free, just like Editors in your workspace. All Editors and Viewers in a workspace are free! Viewers can open any shared link to a doc and view and comment on it. Just like Guest Editors, Viewers are also guests and cannot see the full workspace or folder. What happens when I publish my doc? + You can always share a doc to anyone with a link through the sharing feature inside the doc. When will I get billed for my workspace? + When you sign up for a new plan, as well as when it renews, you will be billed up front for all the users in your workspace. Whenever you add a new paid user to your workspace (or upgrade an existing one), we'll prorate them for the remainder of your billing cycle. On the annual plan, you will be charged monthly for these additional membership changes, with prorations applied for the remainder of the billing year. Additionally, you will be charged whenever your bill exceeds $1,000 from adding new members. Do I get a refund if I remove members from my workspace or cancel my plan? + When you remove members or cancel your plan, we'll apply a prorated credit onto your account you can use for future charges in your workspace. How does Enterprise pricing work? + With increased scalability, security and support, Enterprise plans are a great pick if you're planning to roll out Superhuman Docs across multiple teams or departments. Even on Enterprise, you pay only for Doc Makers. Our pricing scales to fit enterprise teams at every stage and we work with you to find the custom fit for your situation.
